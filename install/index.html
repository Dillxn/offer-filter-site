Install Offer Filter

Offer Filter is a free Android app for Dashers: set your minimums, and it declines Dasher offers below them. Setting it up takes about ten minutes, once, while you’re parked. The screens you’re most likely to see are below, in order.

↓Download Offer Filter

Android 8 or later. A signed APK from Offer Filter’s update server, dash-offer-filter-build.onrender.com, the same place the app gets its own updates.

Before you install

  • It’s a beta. It can misread an offer, decline one you wanted, or, if you turn on Auto-accept, accept one you didn’t want. Check your Dasher history.
  • Declines can lower your acceptance rate. Autopilot’s acceptance-rate goal (new in 0.5.0) helps, but nothing here guarantees a rate or any earnings.
  • It’s independent. Offer Filter isn’t made, endorsed or supported by DoorDash. Using it may conflict with DoorDash’s terms, and DoorDash could limit or deactivate your account.
  • It needs two Android permissions. Accessibility, to read Dasher’s screen and tap Decline, and notification access, to see Dasher’s offer alerts. Peek, on by default, briefly opens Dasher when an offer arrives while another app is in front; you can turn it off.
  • Set it up while parked. Don’t handle your phone while driving.

The app shows its terms and asks you to accept them before it does anything. Privacy explains what it reads and keeps.

Download and install

  1. Download the file

    Tap Download Offer Filter above. Chrome may say File might be harmful (older versions: This type of file can harm your device). Chrome says this about every app that doesn’t come from the Play Store. Tap Download anyway (or OK). Samsung Internet asks a similar question.

  2. Open it

    When the download finishes, tap Open in the message at the bottom of the screen. Missed it? Open Chrome’s ⋮ menu, then Downloads, or your Files app, and tap OfferFilter.apk.

  3. Let your browser install apps, once

    The first time, Android says that for your security your phone isn’t allowed to install unknown apps from this source. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back, and tap Install.

    This applies only to the browser you used. You can turn it off again afterwards: Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps (Samsung: Settings → Apps → ⋮ → Special access → Install unknown apps).

  4. Play Protect

    Google Play Protect may stop to check Offer Filter, because it’s new and doesn’t come from the Play Store. If you see Scan app, tap it: the scan takes a few seconds, and then you can install.

    If Play Protect says the app is harmful, or blocks it, stop there and don’t install it. Please tell us through Feedback so we can look into it. Never turn Play Protect off for Offer Filter.

  5. Samsung Galaxy: Auto Blocker

    If a Galaxy phone says Auto Blocker blocked the install, that’s Samsung’s Auto Blocker, at Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker. While it’s on, it blocks apps from anywhere except Galaxy Store and Google Play, and that includes Offer Filter’s own updates later.

    Turning it off lowers that protection for every app, not only this one. If you’re not comfortable with that, it’s fine to stop here. If you turn it off, Offer Filter can install and update itself; you can turn Auto Blocker back on at any time, but updates will wait until it’s off again.

First run

  1. Read the notice

    Open Offer Filter. Its first screen explains what it reads, the acceptance-rate risk and the terms. Tap I understand and accept only if you agree. Not now closes the app without it doing anything.

  2. Turn on what it needs

    Offer Filter’s home screen lists anything that’s still off, each with a Fix button that opens the right Android screen:

    • Screen reading is off: Accessibility. Choose Offer Filter and turn it on. Android warns that the app can see and control your screen; that’s how it reads offers and taps Decline in Dasher.
    • Background offers are off: Notification access. Choose Offer Filter and allow it. It reads only Dasher’s notifications and ignores every other app.
    • Alerts are blocked: Offer Filter’s own notifications (step 9).

  3. Android 13 and later: “Restricted setting”

    Because the app came from your browser, Android 13 and later keep Accessibility and notification access locked for it at first. If the switch is greyed out, or you see Restricted setting (“For your security, this setting is currently unavailable”), tap OK, then:

    1. Touch and hold the Offer Filter icon and tap App info (or go to Settings → Apps → Offer Filter).
    2. Tap ⋮ at the top right, then Allow restricted settings, and confirm with your PIN or fingerprint.
    3. Go back to Offer Filter, tap Fix again, and turn the switch on.

    Allow restricted settings only appears after you’ve tried the switch once. Do the same if notification access is locked. Android 8 to 12 skip this step.

  4. Allow notifications

    On Android 13 and later, Android asks Allow Offer Filter to send you notifications? Tap Allow. Offer Filter uses them for offers it lets through or can’t read, and for update notices. On Android 8 to 12 they’re on unless you turned them off.

  5. Let Offer Filter install its own updates

    In Offer Filter’s Settings, tap Fix beside Updates can’t install and turn on Allow from this source. This is separate from your browser’s permission in step 3. Updates are signed and checked before they install, and automatic installs wait until a dash ends. Keep the app installed; there’s no need to uninstall to update.

Before your first dash

  1. Set your minimums

    Choose your minimum pay, pay per mile, pay per minute, and pay per item on shopping orders, plus the most stops you’ll take. Offers below a minimum are declined; the rest are left to you. Auto-accept stays off unless you turn it on separately and confirm.

  2. Turn on Autopilot New in 0.5.0

    Autopilot asks what matters more; an acceptance rate of 70% is suggested. It then adjusts how strict the filter is from how often offers arrive, what they pay for your time, and your goal, reading your current acceptance rate from Dasher’s own decline confirmation. Long-press Autopilot to change the goal later. It’s new: watch how it does on your first dashes.

  3. Dash in Earn per Offer

    Offer Filter works with Earn per Offer. Earn by Time isn’t supported in this beta, so pause Offer Filter if you dash by time.

  4. Pick a driving layout

    Set it up before you drive:

    • Dasher full screen, with Offer Filter’s tab on the edge.
    • Google Maps or Waze full screen, with Peek on. For a new offer, Offer Filter briefly opens Dasher, then brings you back to your map.
    • Maps and Dasher in split screen, with the tab on Dasher’s side.

    Offer Filter’s own split view is for planning while parked, not for driving.

While you dash

  1. Pause any time

    Tap the mascot on Offer Filter’s home screen to pause or resume. If anything seems off, pause it and use Dasher directly. To stop completely, turn off Offer Filter’s Accessibility and notification access, or uninstall it.

    New in 0.5.0 During a dash Offer Filter keeps your screen from timing out. The power button still locks it, and it lets the screen sleep when battery saver is on or the battery is low. Peek waits while the phone is locked; unlock while an offer is still up and it picks it back up.

  2. Tell us what happened

    Use Send anonymous feedback in Offer Filter’s Settings (new in 0.5.0), or the feedback form on this site. No account needed. Your phone model and Android version help; leave out customer names, addresses and payment details. We can’t reply to anonymous messages.

What’s in the 0.5.0 beta

  • Autopilot. Adjusts how strict the filter is from how often offers arrive, what they pay for your time, and an acceptance-rate goal you choose at setup (70% suggested), using your real acceptance rate from Dasher’s decline confirmation.
  • Simpler rules. Strict minimums for pay, pay per mile, pay per minute and pay per item on shopping orders, plus a maximum number of stops.
  • Better Peek. It picks an offer back up after you unlock, and your phone doesn’t time out mid-dash.
  • Fewer notifications. No duplicate cards when Dasher’s own notification already has the offer.
  • Feedback without an account. Send it from the app or this site. Optional diagnostics: turn on Attach masked diagnostics for one message, or Share anonymous diagnostics after each dash in Settings. Both start off.

It’s a beta: it can misread an offer, and it makes no promise about earnings or your acceptance rate.
