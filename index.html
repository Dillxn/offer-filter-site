Offer Filter

You set the pace.

Offer Filter is a free Android companion for Dashers. Set your minimums (pay, pay per mile, pay per minute, and items on shopping orders) and the most stops you’ll take. It declines offers below them and leaves the rest to you.

Autopilot, new in the 0.5.0 beta, adjusts how strict the filter is from how often offers arrive, what they pay for your time, and the acceptance-rate goal you choose. One small companion for a long shift.

Android · Public beta

Start while parked.

No account is needed to download the app, get its updates, or send feedback.

  1. Install Offer Filter. The guide walks through Chrome’s download warning, Play Protect, Samsung’s Auto Blocker and Android’s “Restricted setting”, in order.
  2. Open Offer Filter and read the notice, including the acceptance-rate and account risks. Choose I understand and accept only if you agree.
  3. Tap Fix beside each item the home screen lists, and let Offer Filter install its own updates (Settings → Updates can’t install → Fix).
  4. Set your minimums and turn on Autopilot; it asks for an acceptance-rate goal (70% is suggested). Dash in Earn per Offer. Earn by Time isn’t supported in this beta.
  5. Drive with Dasher full screen and Offer Filter’s tab, Google Maps or Waze full screen with Peek, or Maps and Dasher side by side with the tab. Offer Filter’s own split view is for planning while parked.

Something isn’t right?

Tap the mascot to pause, turn off Auto-accept if you turned it on, and use Dasher directly. You can also turn off Offer Filter’s accessibility and notification access in Android settings.
