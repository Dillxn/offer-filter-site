Offer Filter

You set the pace.

Offer Filter is a free Android companion for Dashers. Set three minimums (pay, pay per mile and pay per hour of Dasher’s time estimate) and, if you like, the most stops you’ll take. It declines offers below them and leaves the rest to you. One small companion for a long shift.

New in 0.5.0, the public beta: the three minimums replace the older rules, and optional Autopilot moves how much of your minimums an offer must pay, from how often offers arrive, what they pay for your time and the acceptance-rate goal you choose. Offers it lets through below your minimums are always yours to decide.

Android · 0.5.0 public beta

Start while parked.

No account is needed to download the app, get its updates, or send feedback.

  1. Install Offer Filter. The guide walks through Chrome’s download warning, Play Protect, Samsung’s Auto Blocker (on a Galaxy it can come first) and Android’s “Restricted setting” (on newer Galaxy phones, “App was denied access”).
  2. Open Offer Filter and read the notice, including the acceptance-rate and account risks. Choose I understand and accept only if you agree.
  3. Do the setup steps its home screen lists, in order, with Fix: Allow restricted settings first (Android 13 and later), then Turn on Offer Filter in Accessibility, Allow notification access, Allow alerts and Allow updates. About a day later Android may remind you once that Offer Filter can view and control your screen; that’s expected.
  4. Set three minimums on the constellation: minimum pay, pay per mile and pay per hour, plus max stops if you like. Then tap Auto for Autopilot. It asks What matters more? Keep a top tier (acceptance rate 70% or more, suggested), keep a tier (50% or more) or pay first. Long-press it to change that later. Dash in Earn per Offer; Earn by Time isn’t supported in this beta.
  5. Drive with Dasher full screen and Offer Filter’s tab, Google Maps or Waze full screen with Peek, or Maps and Dasher in split screen with the tab. Offer Filter’s own split screen is for planning while parked.

Peek waits for Dasher to show a background offer, and if one dings while your phone is locked, unlock within 40 seconds and it checks it. During a dash with auto-decline on, your screen doesn’t time out, whatever app is in front; it’s never woken or unlocked, and the power button still turns it off.

Something isn’t right?

In Offer Filter, tap the mascot on its home screen to pause: paused, Offer Filter doesn’t read Dasher at all. Turn off Auto-accept if you turned it on, and use Dasher directly. You can also turn off Offer Filter’s accessibility and notification access in Android settings.
