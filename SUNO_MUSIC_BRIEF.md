## Copy into the music/style prompt

Warm, clear spoken narration over an original miniature electronic score for a warm, beautifully animated Android app film. Organic downtempo electronica with a playful, precise groove; 104 BPM, 4/4, an eight-bar arc. Soft rounded bass, dry wooden clicks, delicate marimba and kalimba plucks, warm electric-piano chords, a few bright glassy notes. Begin with a small curious pulse, then let scattered notes lock satisfyingly into rhythm. Introduce a memorable three-note motif as a friendly little funnel appears. Crisp little accents accompany bouncing paper offer tickets. Open the harmony as a constellation and miniature skyline grow. Finish with a luminous, gentle major chord, a brief sparkling motif, and a clean natural decay. Calm, joyful and intricately composed; intimate scale with a cinematic sense of discovery. Clear transients, spacious mix, no long intro, no huge drop. Aim for an 18.5-second logo-film arrangement with a complete beginning and ending.

No singing, choir, humming, whispers, breath sounds, ASMR, vocal chops, mouth sounds, airy human textures, vinyl hiss, crackle, wind noise, heavy sub drops, harsh cymbals or aggressive pumping. Do not sing the narration. Keep the voice naturally spoken and forward in the mix; duck the music gently under every phrase.
